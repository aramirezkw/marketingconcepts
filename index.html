  7. E7 · Funnel connector. Chevron + conversion rate text between stat tiles (Direction E).
  8. E8 · Kanban column. pageBackground column + 16/20 title + count Chip (Direction E).
  9. E9 · Ad preview. Channel-neutral mock of a social ad inside the builder. Uses only neutral tokens; no third-party brand colors.

Proposed: AI Draft Marker (E2)

Marks anything the AI drafted until the agent reviews it. No new color: it reuses system.action as a 1px dashed border, a neutral subtle Chip with a sparkle icon and the words "AI Draft", and a 12/16 labelTertiary meta line with Accept and Rewrite actions. Once the agent edits or accepts, the border turns solid divider and the Chip goes away. Text carries the meaning, so it never relies on color, and it can't be confused with status Chips because it's always neutral and always says "AI Draft".
